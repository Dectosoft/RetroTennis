HOW TO PLAY

Player Controls
Player 1: W / S (or Mouse)
Player 2: ↑ / ↓ (2P Mode)
Serve / Start: Space or Click
Pause Match: P or Esc
Toggle Audio: M
Restart Match: R
Authentic Angle Mechanics

• Edge Deflection: Hitting the ball near the edge of your paddle deflects it at sharp angles and higher smash speed. Center hits return straight.

• Rally Acceleration: Every successful volley increases ball speed by 3%, pushing reaction times.

• Spin & Momentum: Moving your paddle vertically during ball contact adds spin and vertical impulse.

Game Modes

• 1P vs AI: 4 difficulty tiers ranging from Easy to Master with anticipation.

• 2P Local: Head-to-head 2-player match on the same keyboard or touch screen.

• Rebound Wall: Solo practice mode against a brick rebound court wall.
